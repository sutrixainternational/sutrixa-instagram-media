Hotel GST India, in plain words. Room up to ₹7,500 a night: 5% GST. Above ₹7,500: 18%.
The 5% splits into 2.5% CGST and 2.5% SGST, even for a guest from another state, because the hotel's state is the place of supply.
₹4,000 room: ₹200 GST, which is ₹100 CGST and ₹100 SGST.
