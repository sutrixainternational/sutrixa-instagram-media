How long does one order take in your restaurant? Here's a one-evening table order timing check.
Restaurant owner? Restaurant table turnover starts with this habit, and you can try it this week.

Das table, ek waiter. Order likhne mein hi das minute.

Try it on one busy night:
1. Keep a stopwatch at the pass and time 10 tables.
2. Start when the guests sit. Stop when the order reaches the kitchen.
3. Write each gap and take the average.
4. Mark where it got stuck: waiter busy, menu questions, slip on the counter.
5. Average wait × tables a night = minutes your tables sat unordered.

In our example that's 8 minutes × 30 tables = 240 minutes a night. Your number won't match ours. Do your own.
