GST input tax credit for FY 2025-26 has a last date: 30 November 2026.

Credit kab tak? 30 Nov tak.

Input tax credit is the GST you already paid on purchases. You subtract it from the GST you collected and pay only the difference. Three rules decide if you keep it: the supplier's bill must show in your GSTR-2B, the supplier must be paid within 180 days, and some 5% slabs (standalone restaurants, hotel rooms up to ₹7,500) get no credit at all. Miss 30 November, or your GSTR-9 date if you file earlier, and the credit for that year is gone.

Save this, and send it to whoever files your GST.
