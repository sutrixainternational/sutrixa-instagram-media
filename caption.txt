Where did your paneer go? Here's a 10-minute restaurant kitchen stock check.
It's the cheapest kitchen management habit restaurant owners can start.

Paneer aaya, paneer gaya, hisaab kisi ke paas nahi.

Try it for one week. That's all.
1. Before service, weigh your 5 costliest items.
2. Add today's purchases from the bill.
3. Multiply orders by your recipe portion. 12 paneer tikka at 200 g is 2.4 kg.
4. Count again at closing. Opening plus purchases minus closing is what left the kitchen.
5. Write every throw-away down with a name.

In our example the gap is 1 kg a day. At ₹400 a kilo that is ₹12,000 a month. Yours won't match ours. Do your own.
