Input tax credit is the GST you paid on purchases. You subtract it from the GST you collected on sales.
Collected 1,800 on sales, paid 1,000 on supplies? You pay 800.

3 rules:
1. The supplier's bill has to show in your GSTR-2B.
2. Pay the supplier within 180 days, or reverse the credit.
3. Last date for last year's credit: 30 November, or your GSTR-9 date if you file earlier.

The catch: a standalone restaurant billing 5% gets no input credit, and neither do hotel rooms up to Rs 7,500 a night.
