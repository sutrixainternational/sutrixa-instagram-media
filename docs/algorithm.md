# How Instagram ranks Reels (research, 27 Oct 2026). Binding for every run

## What Instagram itself says (Mosseri, as reported by Buffer and others)
- The top Reels signals are **watch time, likes per reach and sends per reach** (DM shares).
- **Sends matter most for reaching non-followers.** Likes matter slightly more for followers. Make things people would "want to send to a friend".
- **Originality (April 2026):** original creators get the distribution. Accounts that mostly repost are not recommended to non-followers. Never post other apps' watermarks.
- New posts are "auditioned" with non-followers, from small public accounts too. The first 30-60 minutes matter.
- Hashtags don't drive reach (cap 5, used as labels). The keywords people search for belong in the caption, the on-screen text and the profile.
- AI: label photorealistic AI video (we post with isAiGenerated=true). The "AI creator" account label does not change distribution.

## What has been measured (third-party, directional)
- **First 1.7-3s decide it.** Reels with over 60% of viewers still watching at 3 seconds out-reach those under 40% by 5-10x. Average watch is about 8.5s. Ours: 63-80% skip.
- Captions on screen lift completion (about 65% vs 37%).
- Length: 0-30s completes at about 70%+. **30-60s is strong for educational content. 60-90s gets the most saves and shares.**
- Benchmarks for top Reels: shares/reach 2.5%, saves/reach 3.8%, replay rate (plays ÷ reach) over 1.1. A sends/reach of 1-2% is strong, and 3%+ is viral.
- Best windows: Tue-Thu 7-9 PM (we post 7:30 PM IST). Reply to comments in the first hour.

## What this means for every Sutrixa Reel
1. **Informative first (owner rule, 27 Oct: "reels must be informative").** Every Reel teaches at least **3 concrete, checkable things** the viewer can use or tell someone: numbers, steps, names of registers, how GST splits, what to check. Facts must come from docs/products.md or be general, verifiable business knowledge, for example "a hotel room bill splits GST into CGST and SGST, even for an out-of-state guest, because the place of supply is where the hotel stands" (corrected 27 Oct: an earlier example here said out-of-state guests get IGST, which is wrong for room stays; IGST Act s.12(3)). No vague lines like "save time" or "boost efficiency".
2. **Length 30-50s** for teaching Reels. Hold attention with a new visual every 2-4s, never a long static shot. Short 15-20s Reels are allowed only for a single striking proof, at most 1 in 5 posts.
3. **Hook in 1.7s:** the problem is visible in the Gemini clip and stated in 8 words or fewer, and it must score at least 75 on hookscore. Then promise the payoff ("3 registers your CA will ask for").
4. **Built to be sent:** checklists, "send this to your accountant/manager", mistakes to avoid. The ask rotates between send, save and comment KEYWORD (only if the owner handles DMs).
5. **Loop:** the last line points back to the first, so a rewatch reads naturally.
6. **Original only:** our own footage and screens. A reused clip must get a different segment, angle and script. No watermarks.
7. **Captions:** the first line repeats the payoff, then the search phrase in plain words, then the facts as short lines, then one ask. Up to 5 specific hashtags.
8. **Track it:** the weekly review logs shares/reach, saves/reach, the 3s hold if available, and the replay rate per preset.
