Goal set by the owner: 100k followers by 24 Nov 2026 and reach on every Reel. Reported honestly every week. Today: 4 followers. The plan maximises the odds of a breakout. It can't guarantee one.

## Weekly calendar (7:30 PM IST daily)

The weekly review can shift the shares, by at most ±10% a week, towards whatever earns sends and follows.

## Content rules that drive reach (see docs/algorithm.md and docs/lessons.md)
- Teach ≥ 3 concrete facts in 30-50s. Hook ≤ 1.7s. Built to be sent ("send this to your accountant / manager / HR person").
- Every Reel names a specific person to send it to. That is the sends lever.
- Recurring series names: "GST in 40 seconds" (Rizstay), "Missed-call math" (Voxa), "Payroll without Excel" (HR4U), "Marketing for busy owners" (Velocity). Series bring people back and get follows ("follow for part 2").
- End card: "Follow for one business fix a day." Follow asks convert profile visits.

## Distribution levers (the bottleneck, in order of impact)
1. **First-hour sends by the owner and team** to real contacts: hotel owners, accountants, HR people. The strongest signal for a small account.
2. **Collab posts** with clients or partners (shown to both audiences). The owner invites, and we make the Reel.
3. **Comment on 10 larger niche accounts a day**: drafted daily, the owner posts.
4. **Story every day**: repost the Reel, poll sticker.
5. **Consistency**: one Reel every day at 7:30 PM, no gaps.
6. Paid boost (optional, owner decides): a ₹300-500 boost of the best organic Reel to Indian business owners. It can seed the first audition. Not run without the owner's say-so.
