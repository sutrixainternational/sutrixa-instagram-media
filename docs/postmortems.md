# Post-mortems: one per Reel, written by the next day's run before anything new is made

Data: vidiq_instagram_owner_insights (free). Compare against the account median of the last 10 Reels.
For each Reel record: views, watch time, watched %, skip rate (left in the first seconds), likes, comments, shares, saves, follower change.
Then: what went wrong, what to change in TODAY's Reel, and how to get more reach. Every lesson must be applied to today's Reel and checked off in the next entry.

Benchmarks (docs/algorithm.md):
- skip rate under 40% (hold over 60% at 3s);
- watched % over 50% for 30-50s Reels;
- shares/reach 1-2% is strong;
- saves/reach about 3.8%;
- replay over 1.1.

## 2026-10-27 · Rizstay · tips · "Stop adding GST by hand at 11 PM." (80.2) · 46s · DfAApRvjGNV
Numbers about 1 hour after posting: **9 views**, avg watch **23.5s (51%)**, skip **40%**, 0 likes/comments/shares/saves.
Account median of the last 10 (views): about 22. Followers: 4 (no change).

**What went right**
- Best retention the account has had. Skip rate fell from 78% (25 Oct) to 64% (26 Oct) to 40%. Watched % jumped from about 27% to 51% with the informative tips format and kinetic captions.
- Teaching real numbers (the ₹7,500 room-rate threshold, 5% vs 18%) kept people watching.

**What went wrong**
- **Tiny test audience.** Instagram showed it to only 9 people in the first hour. The account has no history of Reels that got sends, so each new post gets a very small audition.
- **Zero sends and zero saves**, so the Reel has no signal to earn a second, bigger audition. The "share this" ask sits at the end, where only about half the viewers get to.
- 40% still leave before 3 seconds. The headline is long (7 words plus the box) and the problem isn't moving in frame 1.

**Change in the next Reel**
1. Put the send ask **in the middle** as well as at the end: "Send this to whoever does your billing", at the strongest fact (the ₹7,500 rule moment).
2. Hook of 6 words or fewer with a number in it, for example "₹7,500 decides your hotel's GST." (score it). The clip must show the problem in motion from frame 0.
3. Keep the tips format and the 40-50s length: retention proves it works.
4. On-screen "SAVE THIS" chip on the most useful fact beat.

**Reach actions (owner + system)**
- Owner: in the first hour, send the Reel by DM to 3-5 hotel or restaurant contacts and to your own staff group. Those sends are the signal that unlocks a bigger audition.
- Owner: repost each Reel to your Story with a poll sticker. Story viewers count as early engagement.
- Owner: reply to every comment within the hour.
- System: keep posting at 7:30 PM daily, and stay on the Rizstay/GST niche for 3-4 days so Instagram learns who the account is for.
